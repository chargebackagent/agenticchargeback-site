Simple, clear pricing

There are two separate costs. We want you to see both before you start.

Paid to us
15% of the disputed amount
  • Charged only if the dispute is won
  • Nothing to us if it is lost or we do not file
  • No setup fee and no monthly fee
Paid to Stripe — win or lose
$15 per dispute (CA$15 in Canada)
  • Stripe charges a dispute fee on every dispute you receive, whether you win or lose. It is not refunded.
  • Stripe charges a second $15 fee when you submit a response. Stripe returns it if you win and keeps it if you lose.
  • These fees are set by Stripe and go to Stripe, not to us. They apply even if you never use AgenticChargeback.

Common questions

Does the AI submit anything on its own?

No. AI helps prepare and draft. Nothing is sent to Stripe until you approve the exact evidence and text.

Do I still pay Stripe’s dispute fees?

Yes. Stripe charges its dispute fee on every dispute, win or lose, and a second fee when you respond (returned if you win). Those fees go to Stripe. Our 15% is separate and charged only on wins. See the example under Pricing.

Who is it for?

Businesses in the United States and Canada that process cards with Stripe. We are strongest for software and subscription businesses.

What if the dispute is lost?

You owe us nothing. We only charge when a dispute is confirmed as won. Stripe’s own fees still apply.

Can you guarantee a win?

No. Card networks and issuing banks decide every outcome. We aim to give you the strongest honest case.

What data do you access?

Only what is needed to build the evidence: the disputed payment and related invoice, customer and subscription records you authorize. Connection tokens are stored encrypted, and we do not sell data. See our Privacy Policy.

What is the current status?

AgenticChargeback is in early access. We are onboarding a small number of merchants, starting with subscription and SaaS businesses.
