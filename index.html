Win back disputed subscription payments.

AgenticChargeback prepares chargeback evidence for SaaS and subscription businesses that take payments on Stripe. You approve every response before it is sent. You pay only when a dispute is won.

What we do

When a customer disputes a payment, the bank sets a deadline and the merchant has to send evidence. Most small businesses do not have time to collect the logins, usage records, invoices and cancellation history that card networks look for. We connect to your Stripe account, collect that evidence, write the response, and put it in front of you for approval. Nothing is sent to Stripe until you approve it.

How it works

1. Connect Stripe
You install our app from Stripe. You choose what to allow and you can disconnect at any time.
2. We prepare
When a dispute opens, we gather the payment, invoice, subscription and usage details that support your case and draft the response.
3. You approve
You review the exact evidence and text. We submit only what you approved, before the deadline.
4. You pay on wins
If the dispute is won, we charge our fee. If it is lost or we do not file, you owe nothing.

What kinds of disputes

  • Fraudulent or unrecognized charges (including "friendly fraud", where a genuine customer disputes a payment they made)
  • Product or service not received
  • Subscription canceled, duplicate charge, and credit not processed
  • Product or service not as described, and general disputes

We will tell you honestly when a dispute is too small or too weak to be worth fighting. Winning is never guaranteed. The card network and the customer's bank decide the outcome.
