Terms of Service

Last updated: October 25, 2026. Early-access terms. We may update them as the service grows, and will post changes here.

The service

AgenticChargeback helps Stripe merchants prepare and submit evidence for card disputes. We submit evidence only after you approve it.

Eligibility

The service is for businesses in the United States and Canada with a Stripe account that you are authorized to connect.

Fees

We charge 15% of the disputed amount for each dispute that is confirmed as won. We charge nothing for lost disputes or disputes we do not file. Stripe's own fees are separate. We will show any change to fees before it applies to new disputes.

No guarantee

Outcomes are decided by the card networks and issuing banks. We do not guarantee that any dispute will be won.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you approve and submit, and for supplying true information. Do not use the service to file false evidence.

Refunds

Because we only charge on won disputes, there is no fee to refund for lost disputes. If you think a fee was charged in error, email us within 30 days and we will review it.

Disconnecting

You can uninstall the app from Stripe at any time. Fees for disputes already won remain payable.

Liability

The service is provided as is, to the extent allowed by law. Our liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
