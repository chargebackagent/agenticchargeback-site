Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026. We may update this policy as the service grows, and will post changes here.

Who we are

AgenticChargeback ("we") provides AI-assisted chargeback evidence preparation and submission for Stripe merchants. Contact: agenticdispute@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Merchant account details: your Stripe account ID, country and business name, and the permissions you grant when you install our app.
  • Dispute data: the disputed payment, amount, reason and deadline, and related charge, invoice, customer and subscription records in your Stripe account.
  • Evidence you provide: files and text you give us for a dispute.

How we use it

Only to prepare, show you, and (with your approval) submit dispute evidence, including drafting text with AI tools; to bill our fee on won disputes; to secure and operate the service; and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your data or your customers' data.

How we protect it

Connection tokens are encrypted at rest. Access is limited to what the service needs. Every submission requires your approval and is logged.

Who we share it with

Stripe (to submit your evidence and read the account data you authorized) and service providers we use to host and run the service, including AI providers used to draft dispute text. We share information when required by law.

Retention and deletion

When you disconnect or uninstall, we delete your connection tokens. You can ask us to delete other stored data, subject to records we must keep by law. Email us to request deletion.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data. We serve merchants in the United States and Canada and aim to follow the laws that apply there.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
